Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this program designed for? ▾
The SAT Blueprint Method™ is built for high school High Schoolers across the U.S. who are serious about reaching a 1500+ SAT score — whether for college admissions, scholarship eligibility, or both. It's especially popular with Florida families targeting Bright Futures and other merit scholarships where score thresholds matter significantly.
What does the 12-week program include? ▾
Every student receives: weekly live group instruction sessions with Patrick, weekly office hours for Q&A and problem review, access to a personalized SAT portal with targeted practice questions and full-length tests, The SAT Blueprint™ (all formulas, strategies, and time management tools), and a fully customized study plan built around their starting score, target score, and next test date.
How is this different from other SAT prep courses or tutors? ▾
Most SAT prep is generic — the same curriculum, the same practice tests, the same advice for every student. The SAT Blueprint is built around your teen's specific weaknesses, timeline, and goals. Patrick works with a small number of students at a time so every family gets real attention and real feedback — not just access to a video library or an overworked tutor juggling 50 students.
Will this work if my teen is already overwhelmed with AP classes and activities? ▾
Yes — that's exactly what the Blueprint is designed for. Rather than piling on hours of unfocused studying, the program teaches your teen to prepare efficiently and strategically. The customized plan ensures every hour is targeted and productive — specifically designed so students don't fall behind in other coursework, burn out, or feel overwhelmed.
How quickly can my teen see score improvements? ▾
Most students begin seeing meaningful improvements within the first few weeks as they fill content gaps and start applying the right strategies. The full 12-week program is designed to deliver the highest possible gains by test day — students see an average improvement of 100–200 points.
How important is the SAT for college scholarships? ▾
Extremely — especially in Florida. Programs like Bright Futures have specific SAT score thresholds that unlock different levels of scholarship funding. A difference of even 40-50 points can mean the difference between a partial and a full scholarship worth thousands of dollars. This is why Patrick builds score targets not just around admissions goals but around the specific scholarship thresholds that matter most to your family.
How much does the program cost? ▾
Pricing is personalized based on your teen's starting score, target score, and timeline. The best way to get accurate information is to book a free Roadmap Session — Patrick will walk you through exactly what the right program looks like for your family and what the investment involves.
