Our learning method

The SAT Blueprint Method™. Assess. Address. Success.

Most prep is more practice without looking at the tape. We find exactly where your teen is losing points, then fix those first.

01

Assess

Identify exactly what’s holding the score back

  • A diagnostic of gaps, strategy mistakes, and timing
  • What the Knowledge and Skills section is already showing
  • A week-by-week plan around the target score and test date

02

Address

Attack the areas costing the most points

  • Small live classes (3–6 students) aimed at those areas
  • The Blueprint’s streamlined formulas and strategies — and when to apply them
  • One-on-one and office hours when something stays stuck

03

Success

Turn practice into confident test-day execution

  • Timed practice tests, analyzed in the portal
  • The portal tracks progress and shows what to work on next
  • Test day feels like one more practice test
