Your teen is working hard. The score isn’t moving.

Most SAT struggles aren’t about intelligence. They’re about structure, strategy, and execution. A lot of prep is more practice without looking at the tape — where the points are actually being lost. The SAT isn’t a test of how smart your teen is. It’s a test of how well they understand the exam: its patterns, its traps, and how to move through it under pressure.

The real risk usually isn’t picking the wrong program. It’s waiting, or doing more of the same, while the test date gets closer.
