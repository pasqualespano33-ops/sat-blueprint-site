Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

My teen tried prep before and it didn't help. Why is this different? ▾
Most prep is generic — same curriculum for everyone, with no real diagnosis of what's costing points. We pinpoint your teen's specific weaknesses and target those first, with real feedback every week.
Is it too late for my teen to improve? ▾
Almost never — the SAT is a learnable test, and students often make their biggest gains in a focused stretch before test day. On your Roadmap Session we'll tell you honestly what's realistic in the time you have.
Will this work if my teen is already swamped with AP classes and activities? ▾
Yes — that's exactly what it's built for. Every hour is targeted and efficient, so your teen prepares strategically without burning out or falling behind elsewhere.
Is the program online or in person? ▾
Fully online over Google Meet — no commute. We're selling results, not a seat next to someone: the live instruction, feedback, and accountability are what move the score.
What if I only want one-on-one tutoring? ▾
There's real one-on-one time built in — a personal onboarding, plus brief one-on-one sessions with Patrick when your teen needs them. Combined with small live groups and a fully personalized plan, they get individual attention where it counts.
How much does it cost — and can Step Up cover it? ▾
Pricing is personalized to your teen's goals and timeline. Florida families who qualify for Step Up For Students (PEP) can often use those funds for our tutoring — we're an approved provider, which can mean little or no out-of-pocket cost. We'll cover the details on your Roadmap Session.
How do I join? ▾
Book a free 45-minute Roadmap Session using the button on this page. Patrick will pinpoint what's holding your teen's score back and outline a clear path forward — no pressure, no obligation.
