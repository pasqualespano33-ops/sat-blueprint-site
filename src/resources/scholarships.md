# SAT Scholarships by State

Some states pay for college when a student hits a published SAT or ACT score. The table on this page lists those statewide merit programs — the SAT line, the award, and a link to the official source.

National Merit is separate: students enter through the junior-year PSAT/NMSQT, not the SAT. Semifinalists later take the SAT or ACT as a confirming score. For the 2027 program, the national Commended cutoff was a Selection Index of 208, and National Merit $2,500 Scholarships are one-time awards. Details: [nationalmerit.org](https://www.nationalmerit.org/).
