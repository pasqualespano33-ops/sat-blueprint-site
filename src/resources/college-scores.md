# SAT Scores by College

Middle-50% SAT ranges at well-known colleges, grouped by score band. Colleges marked as SAT Blueprint alumni are places our students have been confirmed to attend.

SAT totals are the sum of the Reading and Writing and Math 25th and 75th percentiles from the U.S. Department of Education College Scorecard (Fall 2024 entering class). These ranges are not cutoffs — about a quarter of enrolled testers scored below the 25th percentile. The University of California and California State University campuses are test-blind and do not report SAT ranges for admissions. Test policies are for first-year admission in the 2026–27 cycle (fall 2027 entry), unless a later date is noted.
