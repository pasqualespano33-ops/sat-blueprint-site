Program overview

One Program. Twelve Focused Weeks.

The SAT Blueprint Method™ is one program: a 12-week, live online SAT course for high schoolers. It starts with a diagnosis of exactly where your teen is losing points, turns that into a week-by-week plan, and then delivers it through live small-group classes, weekly office hours, a personalized practice portal, and Patrick’s proprietary SAT Blueprint™.

Everything is online over Google Meet, so there’s no commute — and families outside Florida join just as easily as families in Deerfield Beach.

The weekly experience

What Your Teen’s Week Actually Looks Like

Homework and reminders aren’t an afterthought — they’re part of the system. Here’s the loop your teen runs through the program.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    A full-length, timed diagnostic shows exactly where points are being lost — content, strategy, timing, or nerves.

  2. 02

    Personal plan

    Patrick builds a week-by-week plan from your teen’s starting score to their target score and test date.

  3. 03

    Live classes

    Small-group instruction each week over Google Meet, taught in order of SAT priority — with real feedback.

  4. 04

    Homework & accountability

    Targeted practice every week, reviewed — plus reminders, so work actually gets done.

  5. 05

    Practice tests

    Official-style, full-length tests at planned checkpoints to measure progress and sharpen pacing.

  6. 06

    Parent updates

    Parents see how things are going and what’s next — no guessing, no nagging.
