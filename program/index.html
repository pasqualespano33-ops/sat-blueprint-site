Program overview

One program. Twelve focused weeks.

The 12-Week SAT Blueprint Program starts with a diagnosis of exactly where your teen is losing points, turns that into a week-by-week plan, and delivers it through Assess, Address, and Success: live classes of 3–6, one-on-one, weekly office hours, our Digital SAT portal, and practice tests — with accountability, and everything tracked. The weekly experience

What your teen’s week actually looks like.

Homework and reminders aren’t an afterthought — they’re part of the system. Here’s the loop your teen runs through the program.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    A full-length, timed diagnostic shows exactly where points are being lost — content, strategy, timing, or nerves — including what the Knowledge and Skills section already points to.

  2. 02

    Personal plan

    Patrick builds a week-by-week plan from your teen’s starting score to their target score and test date.

  3. 03

    Live classes

    Small-group instruction (3–6 students) over Google Meet, aimed at the areas costing the most points.

  4. 04

    Homework & accountability

    Homework is assigned in the Digital SAT portal, which tracks time studied and what to work on next — so the work actually gets done.

  5. 05

    Practice tests

    Official-style, full-length tests at planned checkpoints, analyzed in the portal and aimed at weak spots, so test day feels familiar.

  6. 06

    Parent updates

    You get a portal login and a weekly progress report, so you always know where your teen stands without having to nag.
