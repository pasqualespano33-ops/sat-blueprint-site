During the call

On your session, Patrick will:

  1. Review where your teen stands today — starting score, next test date, and target.
  2. Pinpoint what’s holding the score back — content gaps, strategy, timing, or mindset.
  3. Discuss realistic score-improvement goals and a timeline around your teen’s schedule and test date.
  4. Identify common mistakes families make with prep and retakes.
  5. Leave you with a clear, focused, personalized plan — whether you enroll or not.

Please attend with your teen

The session is designed for parents of high school students looking for clarity and help with the SAT. Please attend with your teen. If both parents are involved in college-planning decisions, plan to join the session together.
