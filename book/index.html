During the session

On your session, Patrick will:

  1. Review where your teen stands today — starting score, next test date, and target. If you have a score report, we’ll look at the Knowledge and Skills section together.
  2. Pinpoint what’s holding the score back — content gaps, strategy, timing, or mindset.
  3. Give you honest feedback about what’s realistically possible for your teen based on where they currently stand, and talk through a timeline around their schedule and test date.
  4. Identify common mistakes families make with prep and retakes.
  5. Leave you with a clear plan, whether you join or not.

Please attend with your teen

The session is designed for parents of high school students looking for clarity and help with the SAT. Please attend with your teen. If both parents are involved in college-planning decisions, plan to join the session together.

What you can expect

You’ll leave your free Roadmap Session knowing what’s holding your teen’s score back, what’s realistically possible, and what it takes to fix it — whether you join or not. No pressure, and no obligation. You’ll have a concrete plan you can act on, and if SAT Blueprint is a fit, we can talk about how to get your teen started.
