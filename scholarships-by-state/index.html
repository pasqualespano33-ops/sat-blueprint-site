Scholarships & Admissions

SAT scholarships by state.

Some states pay for college when a student hits a published SAT or ACT score. The table on this page lists those statewide merit programs — the SAT line, the award, and a link to the official source.

Data as of November 18, 2026

State merit scholarships with SAT requirements
StateProgramSAT neededAwardOfficial link
Alaska Alaska Performance Scholarship
Details

Student must also complete an APS curriculum. FAFSA priority deadline June 30.

 Level 1: 1210 (or 3.5 GPA); Level 2: 1130 (or 3.0 GPA); Level 3: 1060 (or 2.5 GPA). ACT 25 / 23 / 21 also qualify. Up to $7,000 / $5,250 / $3,500 a year (up to $28,000 total) Official site for Alaska Performance Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Arkansas Academic Challenge Scholarship
Details

Apply by July 1.

 SAT 990 (or ACT 19) if high-school GPA is below 3.00. If GPA is 3.00 or higher, a test score must be on file but no minimum SAT is required. Diploma of Merit also qualifies. At four-year colleges, base awards are $2,000 / $4,000 / $4,000 / $5,000 in years 1–4. Lower amounts at two-year colleges. Need-based PLUS add-ons may apply. Official site for Academic Challenge Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Arkansas Governor's Distinguished Scholarship
Details

Apply by March 1. Qualifying scores must be earned by the end of February. FAFSA required.

 1410 SAT on a single test (or ACT 32 composite or superscore), plus a 3.5 GPA Up to $10,000 a year for tuition, mandatory fees, and room and board Official site for Governor's Distinguished Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Connecticut Roberta B. Willis Need-Merit Scholarship
Details

Counselor submits the application. FAFSA and application due February 15. Cannot be used at Yale or Wesleyan.

 1200 SAT or ACT 25, or rank in the top 20% of the junior-year class. Also need-based (SAI at or below the state's annual cap, recently 10,999). Up to $5,250 a year for a four-year program, or $4,650 for a two-year program (FY26 / 2025-26 figures) Official site for Roberta B. Willis Need-Merit Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Florida Bright Futures — Florida Academic Scholars (FAS)
Details

Also requires a 3.50 weighted Bright Futures GPA, 16 required credits, and 100 volunteer hours (or 100 paid work hours, or a combination totaling 100). File the FFAA between Oct. 1, 2026, and Aug. 31, 2027. Tests by Aug. 31 of the graduation year. Bright Futures combines your best SAT Reading and Writing score and best Math score from any sittings taken by the deadline; scores must be sent directly by the testing agency. Sources: official document and the Bright Futures Student Handbook (official document.

 1330 SAT, ACT 29, or CLT 95 for 2026-27 graduates (class of 2027). For 2027-28 graduates, 1350 SAT, ACT 29, or CLT 96. 100% of tuition and applicable fees at public Florida institutions (private colleges: $212 per credit hour at 4-year schools in 2026-27) Official site for Bright Futures — Florida Academic Scholars (FAS) (opens in a new tab)
Florida Bright Futures — Florida Medallion Scholars (FMS)
Details

Also requires a 3.00 weighted Bright Futures GPA, 16 required credits, and 75 volunteer hours (or 100 paid work hours, or a combination totaling 100). Same FFAA and test-date rules as FAS.

 1190 SAT, ACT 24, or CLT 82 for 2026-27 graduates (class of 2027). For 2027-28 graduates, 1200 SAT, ACT 25, or CLT 83. 75% of tuition and applicable fees at public Florida institutions (100% for associate degrees at Florida College System schools). Private 4-year rate: $159 per credit hour in 2026-27. Official site for Bright Futures — Florida Medallion Scholars (FMS) (opens in a new tab)
Georgia Zell Miller Scholarship
Details

ACT 25 is the posted equivalent of a 1200 SAT for students graduating after Dec. 31, 2023; GSFC can update the ACT equivalent each January. Georgia's HOPE Scholarship (no test score) is separate.

 1200 SAT or ACT 25, from a single sitting, plus a 3.7 calculated HOPE GPA (valedictorian/salutatorian path can qualify without the test score) 100% of standard undergraduate tuition at eligible public Georgia colleges; $2,985 per semester or $2,034 per quarter at eligible private colleges in 2026-27 Official site for Zell Miller Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Kentucky Kentucky Educational Excellence Scholarship (KEES) — test bonus
Details

No separate application. Amounts may change with available funds.

 Bonus starts at SAT 830–870 ($36) and reaches $500 at SAT 1300–1600 (or ACT 28+). ACT 15 is the matching floor. GPA awards of $187–$500 for each high-school year with at least a 2.75 GPA, plus the one-time test bonus, paid each year of college Official site for Kentucky Educational Excellence Scholarship (KEES) — test bonus (opens in a new tab)
Louisiana TOPS (Tech, Opportunity, Performance, Honors, Excellence)
Details

Core GPA and 19 TOPS core units also apply (3.50 core GPA for Honors and Excellence). Qualifying score is due by the April national ACT date of the graduation year. Excellence page: official document

 Tech 920; Opportunity 1030; Performance 1130; Honors 1260; Excellence 1400 (SAT reading + math). ACT: 17 / 20 / 23 / 27 / 31. TOPS does not accept an ACT superscore. Opportunity pays the TOPS tuition amount (varies by campus). Performance adds a $400 annual stipend; Honors adds $800. Excellence pays tuition and fees or $12,000 at public colleges, whichever is less ($8,500 cap at LAICU private colleges). Official site for TOPS (Tech, Opportunity, Performance, Honors, Excellence) (opens in a new tab)
Mississippi Mississippi Eminent Scholars Grant (MESG)
Details

Apply Oct. 1–Sept. 15; supporting documents due Oct. 15. Must attend an approved Mississippi college.

 ACT 29 (ACT superscores accepted) or SAT 1350, or National Merit Finalist or Semifinalist, plus a 3.5 GPA Up to $2,500 a year, not to exceed tuition and required fees Official site for Mississippi Eminent Scholars Grant (MESG) (opens in a new tab)
Mississippi HELP (Higher Education Legislative Plan)
Details

Also requires a 2.5 GPA, Mississippi residency, and FAFSA/Pell eligibility. SAT numeric cutoff: not found.

 ACT 20 required. The official page accepts SAT equivalents from concordance tables; a specific SAT cutoff was not found on the program page. Full tuition for up to four years, for income-eligible students Official site for HELP (Higher Education Legislative Plan) (opens in a new tab)
Missouri Bright Flight (Missouri Higher Education Academic Scholarship)
Details

No application. Scores must be earned by the June test date after graduation (July scores do not count). ACT superscores are accepted. Cutoffs are reset each fall.

 For 2026 graduates: ACT 32+ (top award) or ACT 30–31 (lower award). The state's posted SAT equivalents appear as section scores, so confirm the SAT cutoff with the Missouri Department of Higher Education. Cutoffs for 2027 graduates have not been published yet. Up to $3,000 a year (top score band) or $1,000 a year (next band). Estimated awards currently match those maxima. Official site for Bright Flight (Missouri Higher Education Academic Scholarship) (opens in a new tab)
Nevada Governor Guinn Millennium Scholarship
Details

Also requires the core curriculum and two years of Nevada residency. No application for traditional Nevada high-school graduates; the school submits names.

 SAT 1070 or ACT 21, or a 3.25 GPA (weighted or unweighted) Up to $10,000 total, paid per credit (currently $40–$80 per credit depending on the institution and course level) Official site for Governor Guinn Millennium Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
North Dakota North Dakota Scholarship
Details

Other course, GPA, and Choice Ready requirements also apply. Overview: official document

 SAT 1180 or ACT 24, as part of the state's Choice Ready / scholarship pathways $750 per semester, up to $6,000 Official site for North Dakota Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Oklahoma Oklahoma Rising Scholars Award (formerly Academic Scholars)
Details

Exact dollar amounts on the official page are split across several award components; families should confirm the current package for the campus they plan to attend.

 1540 SAT (critical reading + math) for fall 2027, or ACT sum of 103 on English, Math, and Reading only. National Merit Scholars, National Merit Finalists, and U.S. Presidential Scholars also automatically qualify. Varies by campus. The official page lists award packages by institution type (research universities, regional universities, and two-year colleges) and notes a tuition waiver at public colleges. Official site for Oklahoma Rising Scholars Award (formerly Academic Scholars) (opens in a new tab)
South Carolina Palmetto Fellows Scholarship
Details

Class of 2027 early application deadline May 1, 2027; late deadline July 15, 2027. Counselor page: official document

 1200 SAT or ACT 25, plus a 3.5 SC UGP GPA and top 6% rank; or 1400 SAT or ACT 31 plus a 4.0 SC UGP GPA (no rank required) Up to $6,700 in year 1 and up to $7,500 in years 2–4 Official site for Palmetto Fellows Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
South Carolina LIFE Scholarship
Details

No statewide student application; the college determines eligibility from final records.

 At a four-year college, meet 2 of 3: 3.0 SC UGP GPA; 1100 SAT or ACT 22; top 30% of the graduating class. Two-year colleges: 3.0 SC UGP GPA only. Up to $5,000 a year Official site for LIFE Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
South Dakota South Dakota Opportunity Scholarship
Details

Must be used at an eligible campus in South Dakota. Program overview: official document

 Option 1: 1180 SAT or ACT 24, plus a 3.0 unweighted GPA and required curriculum. Option 2: 1310 SAT or ACT 28 (with ACT college-readiness benchmarks), with no GPA or curriculum requirement. Up to $7,500 over four years Official site for South Dakota Opportunity Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Tennessee Tennessee HOPE Scholarship
Details

File the FAFSA. Tennessee's priority deadline is typically April 1. THEC Family Guide 2026-27: official document

 1060 SAT or ACT 21, or a 3.0 final cumulative GPA (either path qualifies). ACT superscores are not accepted. Up to $2,250 per semester for freshmen and sophomores and $2,850 per semester for juniors and seniors at four-year colleges; up to $1,600 per semester at two-year colleges Official site for Tennessee HOPE Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Texas Texas First Scholarship
Details

This is for early high-school graduates. FY2026 guidelines: official document. 2026-27 dollar amounts follow that year's TEXAS Grant maximum.

 SAT or ACT at or above the 80th percentile, or top 10% class rank, as part of earning a Texas First Diploma and graduating early. The official program page does not publish a fixed SAT number. Equal to a TEXAS Grant: $10,858 if the student graduates two or more semesters early, or $5,429 if less than two semesters early, in the FY2026 (2025-26) guidelines Official site for Texas First Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
West Virginia PROMISE Scholarship
Details

Class of 2027: apply and file the FAFSA by March 1, 2027. Last SAT: June 2027; last ACT: July 2027.

 1080 SAT (EBRW + Math) or ACT 21 using English, reading, and math. Superscores are allowed for the Class of 2027. Also requires a 3.0 GPA. Up to $5,500 a year, or tuition and mandatory fees, whichever is less Official site for PROMISE Scholarship (opens in a new tab)
Wyoming Hathaway Scholarship
Details

SAT numeric cutoffs: not found on the official high-school requirements chart. Program page: official document

 Official 2026 chart lists ACT cutoffs only: Honors 25, Performance 21, Opportunity 19, Provisional 17 (or WorkKeys 12). SAT scores are accepted and converted to an ACT equivalent; official SAT cut scores were not published. $2,360 / $1,770 / $1,180 / $1,180 per semester for Honors, Performance, Opportunity, and Provisional (for courses beginning on or after July 1, 2026). Also requires matching GPAs of 3.5 / 3.0 / 2.5 / 2.5 and the Hathaway Success Curriculum. Official site for Hathaway Scholarship (opens in a new tab)

National Merit is separate: students enter through the junior-year PSAT/NMSQT, not the SAT. Semifinalists later take the SAT or ACT as a confirming score. For the 2027 program, the national Commended cutoff was a Selection Index of 208, and National Merit $2,500 Scholarships are one-time awards. Details: nationalmerit.org (opens in a new tab).
