This Privacy Policy explains how Gradeful Test Prep (“we,” “us”), which offers the SAT Blueprint Method™, handles information collected through this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a Roadmap Session or send a message, we collect what you enter — such as parent/guardian name, phone, email, your student’s first name, grade, test dates, scores, and any notes.
  • Scheduling information. Appointments are booked through our scheduling provider, which receives the details you enter to create your appointment and send confirmations.
  • Basic usage data. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may record standard technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location.
  • Embedded content. Pages may include embedded videos (YouTube) and maps (Google Maps). Those services may collect data according to their own policies when you interact with them.

How we use it

  • To schedule and prepare for your Roadmap Session and respond to your questions.
  • To deliver our services to enrolled families, including progress updates and reminders.
  • To send information about our programs if you’ve agreed to receive it. You can opt out at any time.

What we don’t do

We do not sell student or family personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate (such as website hosting, scheduling, and communication tools), when required by law, or with your permission.

Students

Our services are intended for high school students with the involvement of a parent or guardian. This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through it.

Text messages and email

If you provide a phone number or email, we may contact you about your appointment or our services. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts, or use the unsubscribe link in any email.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we collect and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
