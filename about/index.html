Our story

Replacing guesswork with a proven system.

I’m Patrick Spano — founder of The SAT Blueprint Method™. Alex Torres is our Educational Director.

I played goalie at Yale, then pro hockey in the US and Europe. Now I run Gradeful Test Prep. After helping thousands of families over the years, I’ve learned one thing: most students aren’t stuck for lack of effort. They study hard without knowing what the SAT actually tests, or where the points are going.

A goalie doesn’t get better by playing more games. He gets better by watching the goals he let in. Most SAT prep is the same for everyone — more lessons, more practice tests, and nobody looks at the tape. Alex and I built The SAT Blueprint™ to change that.

More than anything, I want every student to have the chance I did: to walk into the college process with real options, and to pursue the education and future they’re capable of. That’s what this work is really about.

— Patrick Spano

Mission & values

What we believe about the SAT.

Structure, strategy, execution

Most SAT struggles aren’t about intelligence. We replace guesswork with structure, a strategy for every question type, and the practice to execute under pressure.

Diagnosis before practice

We find out exactly where points are being lost — including what the Knowledge and Skills section of the score report already shows — before assigning a single drill.

Live coaching and accountability

SAT specialists and tutors, live classes, one-on-one, office hours, and the Digital SAT portal — with real feedback, and everything tracked. Not a video library.

Opening doors

A score is a means to an end: real college options and scholarship dollars, especially for Florida families pursuing Bright Futures.

How coaching works

Patrick’s method, live every week.

Patrick Spano (founder) and Alex Torres (Educational Director) lead SAT Blueprint and the minds behind its method and the 12-Week SAT Blueprint Program. Patrick runs every Roadmap Session. SAT specialists and tutors deliver the program alongside them: live small-group classes (3–6 students), one-on-one time with Patrick when a student needs it, office hours, the Digital SAT portal, and practice tests — with a week-by-week plan, and everything tracked. Parents get a portal login and a weekly progress report.

Groups are kept to 3–6 students, so your teen is never lost in a crowd.

Where we work

Based in Deerfield Beach. Live online everywhere.

Our office is in Deerfield Beach, Florida, and coaching runs fully online over Google Meet — so families anywhere in the U.S. can join without a commute.
